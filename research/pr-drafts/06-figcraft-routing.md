## Title

boards: route elbow connectors around other objects

## Description

An elbow connector on a board now goes around shapes that are in its way, not only the two it joins.

Fixes #1707.

**What the user saw:** elbows ran straight through shapes that were not their endpoints, and parallel elbows overlapped on shared segments (#1707, retested on 0.15.0 and still broken). #1717 added hand-drawn path handles only.

**Cause:** `board_route::route_points` avoids only the two joined boxes (`docs/boards-parity.md`: "goes around their boxes, 24 units clear").

**What changed, and why this wide**

- `craft-graph-layout` becomes a dependency of `figcraft-doc` and the engine, as a git dependency at the `rev` from #<05>.
- `route_points` takes the other board objects as obstacles and routes with the shared router; parallel connectors are separated. If no route exists it falls back to today's route.
- The engine's derived pass supplies the obstacles; `reshape.rs` keeps hand-shaped elbows as they are.
- Unchanged: straight and curved connectors, hand-shaped elbows, `END_GAP` of 4 pt (#1916), mind-map branch connectors (#2226), connector behaviour on move and undo.
- Docs rows for this capability only (`docs/boards-parity.md` connectors row, `ROADMAP.md` one log line). <!-- fill: split these out of commit 57a1136d -->

**Linked requests:** Fixes #1707. Refs deckcraft#1718 only if DeckCraft or WordCraft later use the router (not part of this change).

**Provenance:** the routing algorithm comes from craft-libs `craft-graph-layout` (papers only, see storytold/craft-libs#<L1>). No other product's behaviour was observed.
